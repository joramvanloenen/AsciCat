Every kitten has a secret.
Nine kittens live in Bramble House.
Tonight, only eight came to dinner.
Explore the rooms. Listen to the kittens.
Use your codebook to untangle their words.
Find the little voice beneath the house.
WASD / arrows to walk. E to talk or inspect.
Touch controls and click-to-walk also work.
Your progress is saved on this device.


TERMINAL FIELD GUIDE
How to be a good detective
@    You, the house's human visitor.
a-h  Eight kittens with encoded messages.
+    A doorway. Walk onto it to change rooms.
?    Something worth inspecting.
:    Carpet. Walk across it.
#    Wall. Admirably solid.

WASD / arrows   Move one character at a time.
E / Space       Talk or inspect nearby things.
P               Pet a nearby kitten.
1 / 2 / 3 / 4   Explore / book / journal / map.
Escape          Close a window or return.

Click a walkable tile to walk there.
Click a kitten or ? to approach and inspect.
Decode the six numbered words. Find a lamp and the cellar key. Then whisper the complete sentence at the cellar door. The codebook has examples; hints are always available. There is no timer or penalty for being wrong.


Start a new night?

            [ THE NINTH KITTEN ]
Some secrets just need a nap.
Behind the door, in a nest of old scarves, sleeps the smallest kitten: Button. The others weren't hiding anything dreadful. They were protecting a very important nap.
Button yawns. Eight kittens tumble into the room. The mansion, for the first time tonight, feels like home.
