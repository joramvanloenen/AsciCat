Select LOOK AT, USE, PUSH, PULL, OPEN, CLOSE, TALK TO, TAKE, PET, or WALK. Then click an object in the map. Actions walk you into reach automatically. The object list beneath the map is an alternative way to select things.
Select USE, choose an inventory item, then click a room object or another inventory item. LOOK AT inspects an item without selecting it. Nothing is consumed by a failed attempt. LOOK reveals physical clues; kittens offer practical hints.
WASD / arrows: move. E: selected action on something nearby. P: pet nearby. L: look. O: open. T: talk. 1-4: panels. Escape: clear the selected item and return.
Voices, replay, SFX, and volume sit above the dialogue. Cats meow occasionally and before and after their conversations.
